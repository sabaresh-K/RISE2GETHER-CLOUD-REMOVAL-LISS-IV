Problem & Technical Architecture

High-Resolution Cloud Obscuration Challenge

ISRO's LISS-IV optical sensor aboard Resourcesat provides exceptional 5.8m spatial resolution. However, persistent tropical cloud cover renders up to 64% of optical scenes unusable, creating critical temporal gaps in national GIS analysis.

⚡ Dual-Engine Neural Architecture

  • Engine 1 (Optical U-Net): Performs rapid single-scene spectral mapping to resolve light haze and thin cloud margins.
  • Engine 2 (SAR-Guided CycleGAN): Fuses Sentinel-1 microwave radar (C-band) backscatter to reconstruct structural terrain hidden beneath 100% dense clouds.

🔄 4-Stage End-to-End Flow Pipeline

  • 01 Data Acquisition: Dual ingest of LISS-IV optical bands (G, R, NIR) and Sentinel-1 SAR (VV/VH dual-pol).
  • 02 Preprocessing & Masking: Automated cloud detection and physical co-registration.
  • 03 Generative Inpainting: Unpaired image-to-image CycleGAN translation with dense U-Net skip connections.
  • 04 GeoTIFF Export: Standard georeferenced rasters preserving coordinate reference systems (CRS).
